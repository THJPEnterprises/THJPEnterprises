How THJP Enterprises Pty Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

THJP Enterprises Pty Ltd (ABN 56 696 226 203) (“THJP”, “we”, “us”) is an Australian importer and trade distributor. We also operate the Vinnie’s Oil Australia and Arborean brands, which have their own websites and policies. This policy covers thjpenterprises.com. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to business enquiries and manage trade accounts. This may include your name, business name, business type, email address, phone number, state or region, and anything you include in a message. We collect it when you use our enquiry form, email us, or deal with us as a stockist, supplier or brand partner.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we use website analytics, it is a privacy-friendly service that counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and assess stockist or brand-partnership applications.
  • To set up and manage trade accounts, orders, supply and support.
  • To send you product, pricing or trade information you have asked for or that relates to your account.
  • To meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

We will not sell your personal information or add you to marketing lists without your consent. You can opt out of any non-essential communication at any time by emailing us.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our website host (GitHub Pages), our form-delivery service (Web3Forms), email providers, and logistics and accounting providers, and only as far as needed for them to do that work. Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How we keep it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing info@thjpenterprises.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
