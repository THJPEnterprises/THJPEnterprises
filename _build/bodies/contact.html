FAQ

Before
you ask.

Can I buy directly from THJP Enterprises?

No — we’re a wholesale importer and distributor. Individual customers can buy our brands from authorised stockists. Vinnie’s Oil stockists are listed at vinniesoilaustralia.com.

What do stockists receive?

Wholesale pricing and margin support, product training, point-of-sale materials, brand assets, a stockist directory listing and a direct account contact.

Do you work with brands outside Europe?

Yes. We work with domestic and international manufacturers of all sizes — what matters is quality, consistent supply and a good fit with the Australian market.

Do you supply New Zealand?

Yes, we supply trade partners across Australia and New Zealand. Get in touch to discuss your region.
