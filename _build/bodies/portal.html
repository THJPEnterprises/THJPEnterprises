Help

Portal
questions.

I’ve forgotten my password.

Use “Reset it here” on the sign-in page and we’ll email you a reset link.

I’ve lost the phone with my authenticator app.

Contact us and we’ll reset your two-factor authentication. You’ll set it up again the next time you sign in.

Can I change my tier?

Tiers are reviewed based on your typical order volume. Talk to us if your ordering has grown.

Which brands can I order in the portal?

Every brand THJP Enterprises distributes, grouped by brand, including bundles and apparel. New brands appear automatically as they join the portfolio.
