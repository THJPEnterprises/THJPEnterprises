Company overview

Built for
the trade.

THJP Enterprises Pty Ltd is an Australian-owned importer, brand manager and wholesale distributor of premium woodworking finishes, tools and supplies.

What we do

The control
company.

We sit between the world’s best manufacturers and the specialist retailers who serve Australia’s makers. We find the products, secure the rights, handle the importation, and build each brand in the local market.

We operate strictly business-to-business. Products reach woodworkers, turners and craftspeople through established local stores staffed by people who know what they’re selling.

Not a bulk timber supplier. Our focus is finishes, tools, specialty timber pieces and value-added maker supplies — distinct from structural timber wholesalers.
