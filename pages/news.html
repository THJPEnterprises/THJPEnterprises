Brand partnershipVinnie’s Oil

THJP Enterprises is the exclusive Australian distributor of Vinnie’s Oil

Vinnie’s Oil was developed in Sweden in 2021 by four master woodworkers. It’s a toxin-free, VOC-free and food-safe hardwax oil system that fully cures in three days. THJP Enterprises holds exclusive distribution rights for Australia and its territories, and supplies the full range through a network of specialist retailers.

The range includes Ultra Penetrating Oil (UPO), UPO Rustic Dark, Eternal Seal, Universal Monocoat, and Hard and Soft Wax, plus 2K system bundles and branded apparel.
