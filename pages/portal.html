What’s inside

Built for
busy counters.

Everything you need to keep the shelves stocked, without phone tag or price-list PDFs going out of date.

Live tier pricing

Your wholesale price is calculated from your account tier and is always current. Higher tiers get better margins, and the top tier gets free shipping.

Order on account

Place purchase orders online with GST calculated for you. Apparel orders support size breakdowns. We confirm shipping after you submit.

Order history

See every order you’ve placed and its status, from submitted to shipped.

Dealer resources

Download Safety Data Sheets, spec sheets, marketing and point-of-sale kits, and training material.

Product image gallery

High-resolution product photography for your website, socials and in-store displays.

Secure by design

Every account is approved by our team and protected with two-factor authentication. Pricing is never visible to the public.

Getting access

Four steps
to ordering.

Portal accounts are for approved THJP Enterprises stockists only. There’s no public sign-up.

01

Apply

Tell us about your business using the stockist application form.

02

Approval

We review your application and set up your trade account and tier.

03

Invite

You receive an email invitation to set your password.

04

Secure & order

Set up two-factor authentication on first sign-in, then start ordering.

Help

Portal
questions.

I’ve forgotten my password.

Use “Reset it here” on the sign-in page and we’ll email you a reset link.

I’ve lost the phone with my authenticator app.

Contact us and we’ll reset your two-factor authentication. You’ll set it up again the next time you sign in.

Can I change my tier?

Tiers are reviewed based on your typical order volume. Talk to us if your ordering has grown.

Which brands can I order in the portal?

Every brand THJP Enterprises distributes, grouped by brand, including bundles and apparel. New brands appear automatically as they join the portfolio.
